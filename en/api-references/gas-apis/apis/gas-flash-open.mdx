---
title: Energy Flash Recharge
api: POST /api/tron/gas/flash/open
description: "Create a TRON energy flash recharge order"
---

Energy flash recharge always uses `service_charge_type=10000`. Do not pass `service_charge_type` in the request.

<Note>
  - Only activated TRON addresses that meet the flash recharge rules are supported. Orders are rejected if the purchased quantity is too low or the address's current energy status does not qualify.
  - Orders are processed asynchronously. A `trade_no` is returned once the local order is created and the funds are locked. Order progress and settlement details are available in the `swap_info` field of the [record list](/api-references/gas-apis/apis/gas-record-list) endpoint.
  - **Fund locking**: When the order is created, a fee is locked based on the estimated actual delegated energy plus a safety margin, using the energy price and user fee rate at order time. The locked amount may be slightly higher than the fee estimated from `energy_num`.
  - **Settlement**: After the energy is reclaimed, the actual fee is calculated from the final billed energy quantity confirmed by the system, using the price and fee-rate snapshot taken at order time. The fee is deducted from the locked amount, and the remainder is automatically unlocked back to the available balance.
  - **Order failure**: The entire locked amount is unlocked back to the available balance and no fee is charged.
</Note>

#### Request Parameters

<ParamField query="request_id" type="string" required>
  Unique business request ID. It cannot be reused under the same account.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="receive_address" type="string" required>
  Address receiving the energy. Only one address is supported.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="energy_num" type="integer" required>
  Energy quantity purchased by the user. Final billing is based on the actual billed energy quantity confirmed by the system after reclaim, which may differ slightly from the purchased quantity. See `swap_info` in the record list endpoint for settlement details.
</ParamField>
#### Response Parameters

<Expandable title="object">
  <ResponseField name="trade_no" type="string">
    Gas Station order ID.
  </ResponseField>
</Expandable>
